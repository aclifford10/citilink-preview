Automatic translation

CITILINK translates chat messages into each reader’s language using automated systems, including artificial intelligence (“AI translation”). Translated messages are labelled “Translated · See original”.

AI translation can be incomplete or inaccurate, and can miss tone, humour, slang or cultural context. It is provided for convenience. The original message is always available through “See original”, and if they differ, the original is the authoritative version.

Don’t rely on AI translation for legal, medical, financial, safety-critical or emergency matters, or to give or understand consent. Each sender is responsible for the message they wrote. [Counsel: check against KSchG and the DSA.]

Automatic translation can be switched off in My profile at any time. Bad translations can be reported from the message menu.
