Safety at CITILINK

Built for groups of friends, so nobody walks in alone.

Groups, not strangers

Everyone joins with friends. There are no one-to-one messages between strangers and no cold DMs. Groups say hi to groups, and a shared chat only opens when both say yes.

Private or Public

Private groups are invite-only and never show up in search. Public groups can be found by other groups, who can send an intro. Members choose together.

Verified phone numbers

Every account signs in with a verified phone number. Nobody ever sees anyone else’s number.

Text only at launch

Chats are text only, so nobody can be sent unwanted images.

Remove, report, block

Any group can remove a member instantly. Report or block from any message, profile or group. Urgent reports are reviewed by a person.

City only, never location

Others see a city, never an address or a live location.

Meeting up

Meet in public places, as a group, and tell someone where you’re going.

In an emergency

Call 112 (EU emergency) or 133 (Austrian police). CITILINK is not an emergency service.
