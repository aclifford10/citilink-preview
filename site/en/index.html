How it works

Groups meet groups.

  1. 1

    Start a group

    2 to 5 friends. Choose Private (invite-only, not searchable) or Public (other groups can find it).

  2. 2

    Say hi to a public group

    Send an intro from the whole group. If they say yes, a shared chat opens for both groups.

  3. 3

    Chat, then meet

    Text chat in any language, plan something together, go. Everyone you meet came with friends who chose them.

Surprise Link!

Every Friday, private groups that opt in get linked with a surprise group for the weekend. Off unless the whole group turns it on.

Safety

Arrive together. Leave with more friends.

No cold DMs

Nobody can message anyone one-to-one out of nowhere. Groups talk to groups.

Verified phone numbers

Every member signs in with a verified number. Numbers are never shown to anyone.

Chosen by friends

Everyone you meet is in a group with friends who invited them. Any group can remove someone instantly.

Text only at launch

No photos or videos in chats, so nothing unwanted lands. Report or block from any message.
